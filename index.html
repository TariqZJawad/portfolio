Research & Publications

Ergotropy Protection via Cavity Detuning in Collective Open Quantum Batteries

Submitted — EPJ D, 2026
Sole author

Derives a closed-form scaling law, Δ*(N) ∝ √N, for passively protecting an N-qubit quantum battery from superradiant decay by detuning an intermediate cavity — no external driving energy required. Verified with full QuTiP simulation of the collective open-system dynamics.

arXiv:2605.04042 ↗ Simulation code ↗

Impact of Vacuum Fluctuations on the Properties and Stability of CdSe Quantum Dot Assemblies

Submitted — Indian Journal of Physics, 2026
Co-authored with F. A. Al-Temimie & A. A. Jabbar

Combines DFT electronic-structure calculations (GPAW) with Lifshitz theory to quantify Casimir–Lifshitz forces and quantum-tunneling energy splitting in nanoscale CdSe quantum dot clusters — and shows vacuum forces alone can't drive their self-assembly.

Cavity-Magnon-Phonon Quantum Battery Beyond the Rotating-Wave Approximation

In preparation
International collaboration — India & China

Studies Liouvillian exceptional points and non-Markovian ergotropy dynamics in a cavity-magnon-phonon platform, extending the detuning-protection idea beyond the rotating-wave regime.
